Big Fish

A goofy and recognisable landmark, this 10 metre long ceramic salmon lays on the edge of the river Lagan at Donegal Quay. Designed by artist John Kindnes, it tells a story of belfast through its mosaic tiling. Each scale showing a piece of history, from historic maps to newspaper clippings and childrens' artwork. a beautiful and iconic timecapsule of Belfast and its people. This piece was inspired by the irish myth of the salmon of knowledge, tying folklore into a cultural monument.

Cave hill Country park

An iconic natural landmark, rising above the city, and offering panoramic views accross Belfast Lough. its most famous feature is Napoleons Nose, a cliff said to have inspired Johnathan Swift during the writing of Gulliver's Travels. This beautiful park provides a wonderful and beautiful Hike, with ancient sites dotted across its path.
